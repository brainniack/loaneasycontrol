Loan Easy Control Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026 · Versión en español

Summary

Loan Easy Control is a record-keeping tool for people and small businesses that lend money. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data. The app has no accounts and no servers: everything you enter is stored only on your device.

Information you enter

The clients, loans, payments, expenses, cash register records, users and audit log you create are saved in the app's local storage on your device. We cannot see or access this information.

Passwords

Passwords for the app's internal users are stored on the device using a salted hash. They are never sent anywhere.

Password recovery

Each user registers a WhatsApp phone number so the password can be recovered; this data stays only on the device. A recovery request generates a temporary password and opens WhatsApp on your device with the message ready; you tap send. The app sends no data to our servers or to third parties and makes no internet connection for this. WhatsApp is governed by its own privacy policy.

Backups, reports and messages

Backups and exported reports (PDF, Excel, CSV) are saved only where you choose (for example, Files, iCloud Drive or email). The "Send" buttons open WhatsApp, Messages or Mail with a prepared text; you review and send it yourself.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play or Apple (App Store), depending on your device. We do not receive your payment information. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Play Store › Payments & subscriptions, or in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on iPhone.

Permissions

The app may ask for access to your photos only if you choose to add your business logo. The image stays on your device.

Analytics and advertising

The app does not include analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data at any time in Settings › Delete all data, or by deleting the app from your device.

Children

The app is intended for businesses and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
